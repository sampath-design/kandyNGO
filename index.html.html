Frequently Asked Questions

Guidance regarding NGO registration, renewal fees, and district approvals.

How long does the NGO registration process take?

Verification by the Divisional Secretariat and police security clearance typically takes 2 to 4 weeks upon submitting all required documentation.

Is annual renewal mandatory for Community Based Organizations?

Yes. Under Voluntary Social Services Act guidelines, annual progress reports and audited financial accounts must be submitted by March 31st each year.
